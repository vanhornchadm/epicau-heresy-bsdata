# Fan-made candidates to revisit

These units and rules come from Empire of Ashes ("Epic 30K AB", Public v1.0), a separate fan ruleset built on EpicAU. They're left out for now: most are Empire of Ashes' own designs with no Epic or Legions Imperialis source, overlap something we already have, or need units we don't have.

The Empire of Ashes points below are for reference only. If one is added, convert it against existing EpicAU units and price it within our lists, as with the other fan-made units in the README.

Already converted from this list: the Legion Air Defence Battery, the Kratos as a Legion Tank upgrade choice and the Militia Flak Battery. Empire of Ashes' Castraferrum Dreadnought Talon is our existing Legion Dreadnought Talon.

## Legion Astartes core list

- [ ] **Skyreaper Defender:** the other choice in Empire of Ashes' Air Defence Battery (3 for 250, 0-1 per 2000). It's AV 30cm 5+/6+/5+ with a Skyreaper Battery (30cm, 2× AP4+/AT6+/AA5+). We have no such unit, only the Mastodon's Skyreaper Battery (30cm, 2× AP4+/AT5+/AA5+). It could be a second choice for our Air Defence Battery, on a Rhino-sized hull with the Mastodon's battery.
- [ ] **Inductii Squad:** 8-12 recruits at 225, +20 each. They're weaker Tactical Marines with -1 to action and rally tests. There's little reason to take them over Tacticals.
- [ ] **Scout Detachment:** 6 Scouts with Drop Assault, 225. This overlaps our Reconnaissance Squad (4 for 150).
- [ ] **Veteran Squad:** 6-8 at 300, +50 each, 0-1 per 3000, INF 15cm 4+/4+/3+. This overlaps the Legion-specific elite units.
- [ ] **Plutona Assault Drill:** a one-off 300-pt war engine. It's WE 10cm 4+/5+/4+, with a siege melta array and 2 twin heavy flamers. It would need a transport capacity and the Tunneler rule, like our Termite.

## Legion-specific units

All of these are Empire of Ashes designs.

- [ ] **Deathwing Companions (Dark Angels):** 4 for 340, 0-1 per 2000. They're INF 15cm 4+/3+/4+ with a Calabanite warblade (MW, Lance, EA(+1)) and Remorseless, and they count as Terminators for transport.
- [ ] **Sunkiller Squad (Emperor's Children):** 4 for 250, 0-1 per 2000, INF 15cm 4+/5+/5+. Each carries 2 lascannons (45cm, AT5+, Ignore Cover).
- [ ] **Fenrisian Wolf Pack (Space Wolves):** 6 Fenrisian Wolves plus 2 Grey Slayers for 275. The wolves are INF 15cm 6+/4+/-.
- [ ] **Luperci Pack (Sons of Horus):** 6 Luperci Possessed for 300, 0-1 per 2000. They're INF 15cm 4+/4+/5+ with talons (Fleshbane, EA(+1)), Berserk, Ferocity, Fearless and an Invulnerable Save, and they can't claim objectives.
- [ ] **Chogorian Skyhunters (White Scars):** 6 Scimitar Jetbikes, any of which may become Land Speeders or Golden Keshig. This is close to our Legion Sky Hunter Attack Squadron (5 for 175).

## Solar Auxilia

- [ ] **Carnodon Squadron:** 5 for 250, +50 each up to 8. It's AV 30cm 4+/6+/5+ with a twin lascannon or volkite culverin, plus sponsons. It isn't in Legions Imperialis. It would fit on the Leman Russ hull at about 50 each.

## Imperial Militia

- [ ] **Destroyer Tank Hunter Squadron:** 3 for 200, 0-1 per 2000. It's AV 25cm 4+/6+/6+ with a heavy laser destroyer (75cm, AT3+, Armourbane). NetEA's Imperial Guard lists have a Destroyer Squadron at the same 3 for 200, which would make a better source. The Militia has no tank hunter, but Solar Auxilia allies have the Valdor.
- [ ] **Salamander Scout Squadron:** 3 for 125, LV 30cm 6+/6+/5+. Check the NetEA Imperial Guard lists for a profile first.
- [ ] **Determined and Void Fighters Provenances:**
  - **Determined:** +1 FF when defending in an assault or giving supporting fire.
  - **Void Fighters:** Grenadiers gain Jump Packs and 30cm movement but may only use the Arvus Lighter as a transport.

## Mechanicum Taghmata

- [ ] **Ark Mechanicus:** a spacecraft taken as a Lord of War, 250, 0-1 per army. It's flavour only.

## Knight Household

- [ ] **Mechanicum Lance:** 3 Mechanicum House Knights for 325. It needs Mechanicum Knight units, which we don't have.
- [ ] **Grand Questoris Lance:** 375, +125 each up to 4.
- [ ] **Martian Support upgrade:** adds Mechanicum Knights or 1-3 Moirax (+50).

## Legio Custodes

- [ ] **Erinyes Jetcycle Detachment:** 5 Sisters of Silence jetcycles for 200. They're INF 35cm 5+/5+/3+ with snareguns and neurolashes.
- [ ] **Equinox Interceptor Flight:** 2 for 325, an AC fighter with 5+ armour. Custodes have no fighter of their own.
- [ ] **Kheron Acquisitor:** a transport upgrade (+65), AV 25cm 5+/6+/4+.
- [ ] **Oblivion Knight:** a character upgrade (+50) for Sisters formations, with an executioner blade (Fleshbane, Sniper, EA(+1)), Commander, Inspiring and a 5+ Invulnerable Save.
- [ ] **Vault Access:** upgrades a Galatus to a Telemon for +25. Our Morotoi already offers the Telemon.

## Legio Titanicus

- [ ] **Warhound weapons:** Swarmer Missiles, Ursus Claw and Melta Lance. They're not in EpicAU or Legions Imperialis.

## Optional rules

- [ ] **Legion traits** for all 18 Legions, and **army traits** for the other lists:
  - **Solar Auxilia:** Rally 'round the Flag.
  - **Knights:** Second Greatest Predator.
  - **Titanicus:** Heart of Steel and Warhorn.
  - **Mechanicum:** Targeting Algorithm.
  - **Custodes:** Hunters of the Throne.
  - **Daemons:** Purposeful.

  These are Empire of Ashes' design choices, not unit data. Several Legion traits don't work as written:
  - **Ultramarines:** the trait doesn't make sense.
  - **Emperor's Children:** the re-roll.
  - **Iron Warriors:** Siege.
  - **Word Bearers:** refers to "wounds".
  - **Thousand Sons:** the invulnerable save.

  Any of them would need rewriting before it's added as an optional rule.
