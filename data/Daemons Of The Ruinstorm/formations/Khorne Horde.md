# Khorne Horde

One Bloodletters unit carries the Herald; may add Lesser Daemons, Daemons and 1 Greater Daemon.

## Composition

- 6 [[Bloodletters]]
- 1 [[Herald of Khorne]]
- **Additional Lesser Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Bloodletters]], +25 pts each
  - [[Flesh Hounds]], +25 pts each
- **Additional Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Bloodcrushers]], +50 pts each
  - [[Skull Cannon]], +50 pts each
- 0-1 [[Bloodthirster (Greater Daemon)]], +200 pts each
