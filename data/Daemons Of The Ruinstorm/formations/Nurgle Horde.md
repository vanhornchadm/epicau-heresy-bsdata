# Nurgle Horde

One Plaguebearers unit carries the Herald; may add Lesser Daemons, Daemons and 1 Greater Daemon.

## Composition

- 6 [[Plaguebearers]]
- 1 [[Herald of Nurgle]]
- **Additional Lesser Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Plaguebearers]], +25 pts each
  - [[Nurglings]], +25 pts each
- **Additional Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Beasts of Nurgle]], +50 pts each
  - [[Plague Drones]], +50 pts each
- 0-1 [[Great Unclean One (Greater Daemon)]], +200 pts each
