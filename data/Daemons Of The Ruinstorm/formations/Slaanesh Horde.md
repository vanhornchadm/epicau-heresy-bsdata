# Slaanesh Horde

One Daemonettes unit carries the Herald; may add Lesser Daemons, Daemons and 1 Greater Daemon.

## Composition

- 6 [[Daemonettes]]
- 1 [[Herald of Slaanesh]]
- **Additional Lesser Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Daemonettes]], +25 pts each
  - [[Seekers]], +25 pts each
- **Additional Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Seeker Chariot]], +50 pts each
  - [[Fiends]], +50 pts each
- 0-1 [[Keeper of Secrets (Greater Daemon)]], +200 pts each
