# Tzeentch Horde

One Horrors unit carries the Herald; may add Lesser Daemons, Daemons and 1 Greater Daemon.

## Composition

- 6 [[Horrors]]
- 1 [[Herald of Tzeentch]]
- **Additional Lesser Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Horrors]], +25 pts each
  - [[Flamers]], +25 pts each
  - [[Screamers]], +25 pts each
- **Additional Daemons**: 0-6 in any mix of:
  - [[Burning Chariot of Tzeentch]], +50 pts each
- 0-1 [[Lord of Change (Greater Daemon)]], +200 pts each
