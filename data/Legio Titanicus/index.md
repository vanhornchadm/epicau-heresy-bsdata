# Legio Titanicus

## Notes

- Each upgrade may only be taken once per formation.
- Legio Titanicus has no Support Detachments.
- Warhounds carry 2 Scout Titan weapons; Reavers 3 and Warlords 4 Scout or Battle Titan weapons. The Warbringer carries a Quake or Volcano Cannon plus 2 weapons, the Warmaster 1, the Dire Wolf 1 heavy weapon and the Iconoclast a Kirus siege drill or grav imploder.
