## Notes

DC 12, 8 Void Shields, Cortex Controller, Fearless, Inspiring, Reinforced Armour, Walker, Secondary Targeting Protocols. May step over impassable terrain or dangerous terrain that is lower than the titan's knees and no greater than 2cm wide. Critical Hit: Roll a D6 in the end phase for the remainder of the game, on a 1, the Titan's reactor explodes, destroying it completely and hitting all units within 5cm on a standard 4+ to hit, on a 2-3, the Titan loses a point of DC, 4-6 the reactor is repaired.
