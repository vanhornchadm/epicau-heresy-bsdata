## Notes

Slow and Steady: May not be used on the first turn of a battle unless the scenario specifically says otherwise. Transport: May carry 60 of the following units; Tactical, Breacher, Assault, Rapier, Outrider Bike, Terminator. Additionally, 60 of the following units; Dreadnoughts, Rhinos, Land Raiders, Whirlwinds, Basilisk, Medusa, Sicaran, Predators or Vindicators; plus 9 Thunderhawks, 4 Storm Birds and enough Assault Rams, Assault Claws, Drop Pods or Storm Eagles to carry any other units on board.
