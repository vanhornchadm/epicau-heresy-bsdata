# Drop Pod

## Profile

| Type | Speed | Armour | CC | FF |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Special | - | - | - | - |

## Weapons

| Weapon | Range | Firepower |
|---|---|---|
| Deathstorm Bombardment | 15cm | AP5+/AT5+ |

## Notes

Planetfall, Transport: May carry 1 formation that only includes the following units Tactical, Breacher, Assault, Rapier, Dreadnought. Once landed resolve any attacks against all units within 15cm of the Drop Pod. Units transported disembark and must be placed within 5cm of the Drop Pod or within 5cm of another unit from the same formation that has landed, as long as all units remain withing 15cm of the Drop Pod. Once landed, the Drop Pod has no further effect.
