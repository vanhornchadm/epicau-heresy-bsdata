|---|---|---|---|---|
| AV | 30cm | 4+ | 6+ | 3+ OR / 5+ |

## Weapons

| Weapon | Range | Firepower |

| Flamestorm Cannon | 30cm AND (15cm) | AP3+, Ignore Cover AND (small arms) Ignore Cover |
| AND | AND | AND |

## Notes

A Legion Predator must select either a TL Lascannon OR Predator Cannon OR Flamestorm Cannon. A Legion Predator must also select either Heavy Bolters OR Lascannons OR Heavy Flamers for its side sponsons. Heavy Flamers and Heavy Bolters convey a FF value of 3+. You must select all weapon options before the start of the game.

## Weapon options

- **Turret**: pick one
  - TL Lascannon
  - Predator Cannon
  - Flamestorm Cannon
- **Sponsons**: pick one
  - 2 x Heavy Bolters, FF 3+
  - 2 x Lascannon, FF 5+
  - 2 x Heavy Flamers, FF 3+
