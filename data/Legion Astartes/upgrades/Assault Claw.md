# Assault Claw

Add enough Kharybdis Assault Claws to transport the entire formation. Each carries 4 Tactical-sized units, 2 Terminators or 1 Dreadnought; the count is set from the formation.

## Adds

- Transports carrying 4 Assault Claw space each: [[Kharybdis Assault Claw]], +75 pts each
