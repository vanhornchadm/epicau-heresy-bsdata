# Heavy Transport

Add enough transports to carry the entire formation. Land Raiders carry 2 Tactical-sized units or 1 Terminator, Spartans twice that and Mastodons four times (Dreadnoughts count as Terminators); the count is set from the formation.

## Adds

- **Transport**: one of:
  - **Land Raider Phobos**:
    - Transports carrying 2 Transport space each: [[Land Raider Phobos]], +75 pts each
  - **Land Raider Proteus**:
    - Transports carrying 2 Transport space each: [[Land Raider Proteus]], +75 pts each
  - **Spartan Assault Tank**:
    - Transports carrying 4 Transport space each: [[Spartan Assault Tank]], +125 pts each
  - **Mastodon Heavy Assault Transport**:
    - Transports carrying 8 Transport space each: [[Mastodon Heavy Assault Transport]], +200 pts each
