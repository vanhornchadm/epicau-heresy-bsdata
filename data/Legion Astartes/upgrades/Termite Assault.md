# Termite Assault

Add enough Termite Assault Drills to transport the entire formation (2 Tactical-sized units or 1 Terminator each); the count is set from the formation. A formation carried entirely in Termites may arrive using the Tunneler rule.

## Adds

- Transports carrying 2 Transport space each: [[Termite Assault Drill]], +30 pts each

## Sources

- Legions Imperialis (BSData): converted against EpicAU transports
- Net Epic Armageddon rulebook: the Tunneler rule (2.1.23), as on the Squat Termite
