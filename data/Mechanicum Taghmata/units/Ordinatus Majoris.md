## Notes

DC4, 4 Void Shields, Inspiring, Reinforced Armour. An Ordinatus Majoris is armed with a Sonic Disruptor OR Golgothan Missiles OR a Nova cannon; choose one option before the game. Critical Hit Effect: The Ordinatus is destroyed. All units within 15cm suffer a hit on a 5+. All friendly formations within line of sight take a Blast Marker.
