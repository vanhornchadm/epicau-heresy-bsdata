# Transport

Add enough transports to carry the entire formation (Thallax, Ursarax and Myrmidons take 2 spaces, other infantry 1; Land Raider 2, Triaros 4); the count is set from the formation.

## Adds

- **Transport**: one of:
  - **Triaros Armoured Conveyor**:
    - Transports carrying 4 Transport space each: [[Triaros Armoured Conveyor]], +75 pts each
  - **Mechanicum Land Raider**:
    - Transports carrying 2 Transport space each: [[Mechanicum Land Raider]], +75 pts each
