## Notes

Self-Planetfall, Transport: May carry 1 formation. When landing a Drop Rok, roll twice for scatter, a Drop Rok may land in dangerous terrain, but not impassable terrain. If the Drop Rok lands in impassable terrain it and its passengers are destroyed. Units transported disembark and must be placed within 5cm of the Drop Rok or within 5cm of another unit from the same formation that has "landed", as long as all units remain within 15cm of the Drop Rok. Units disembarking from a Drop Rok cannot be targeted by a unit on Overwatch. Once landed, the Drop Rok becomes impassable terrain and has no further effect.
