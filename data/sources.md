# Sources

Where the units and rules come from. Each entry added beyond the EpicAU wiki lists its sources in a "Sources" section, by the names below.

## EpicAU wiki (archived)

<https://web.archive.org/web/20201111221247/http://epicau.com/mw/index.php/Main_Page>

The EpicAU community's Horus Heresy supplement for Net Epic Armageddon, which this data is built from.

## Net Epic Armageddon rulebook

<https://tp.net-armageddon.org>

The NetEA core rules that EpicAU builds on.

## Departmento Munitorum

<https://munitorum.com>

An online Epic army builder, used where the archived wiki has gaps.

## Legions Imperialis (BSData)

<https://github.com/BSData/Horus-Heresy-Legions-Imperialis>

Games Workshop's Horus Heresy Epic-scale game, read from the BSData data that New Recruit uses. Its units are converted against comparable EpicAU units.

## NetEA Imperial Guard lists

<https://tp.net-armageddon.org>

The NetEA tournament pack Imperial Guard army lists.

## Epic-UK army lists

<http://epic-uk.co.uk/wp/army-lists/>

The Epic-UK community codices for Epic Armageddon.

## Dakka Dakka Kratos proposal

<https://www.dakkadakka.com/dakkaforum/posts/list/812078.page>

A forum proposal for an Epic Kratos squadron, last edited in November 2024.

## Empire of Ashes

A separate fan ruleset built on EpicAU ("Epic 30K AB", Public v1.0). Its candidates are in the backlog.

## Derived from EpicAU units

Built from existing EpicAU units and weapons, with points set against comparable units.

## Placeholder

Entries whose stats, rules or points were adapted from Legions Imperialis or Empire of Ashes. That data has been removed until it is rewritten, and they keep only their names, unit types, weapon names and place in the lists.
